Scope and evidence

Editorial A1–B2 study selection: adverbs plus clearly labeled adverbial uses, focus words and fixed expressions. A scoped 36-entry benchmark was selected after reviewing all 35 pages of Sicher! B2 Lernwortschatz. It is not every adverbial expression in that source, nor a complete B2 lexicon. Goethe B1 was checked for exact word/phrase occurrences in extracted text, not exhaustively audited by part of speech. Occurrence is not proof of the selected sense or CEFR level. Two corrupted strings in the Hueber text were excluded. Most adjective-as-adverb possibilities and paired conjunctions are outside the benchmark. All German examples, Egyptian Arabic translations and image prompts are original study aids, NOT dictionary or publisher quotations.

Hueber Sicher! B2 vocabulary · Goethe B1 vocabulary · IDS connector grammar

Learning checks

Contrast schon / noch / erst; noch nicht / nicht mehr; gern / gut; pünktlich / rechtzeitig; anscheinend / scheinbar; umsonst / vergeblich; and connector verb-second order versus subordinating conjunctions. Pictures are mnemonic clues, not exact definitions—especially for abstract connectors. The Egyptian cue disambiguates the selected sense.

Recall the expression before Reveal, listen to the example, then produce your own sentence. This deck complements the adjective deck; it does not promise all senses or B2 mastery by memorization alone.
